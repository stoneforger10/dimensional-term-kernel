## Notes / Description (997/1000)

DimensionalTermKernel is a reusable exact-rational quantity algebra. GenLayer validators independently fetch a complete commit-pinned unit-definition document, verify full-response SHA-256 and infer every dimension exponent, scale, origin offset and absolute-point versus difference kind. Exact normalized report equality is required. Any missing, ambiguous or failed evidence holds the entire unit set. Consumers bind the immutable environment root. Typed arithmetic rejects mixed dimensions, point-plus-point operations, point products and incompatible conversions; valid calculations retain exact fractions. Native principal/nonce bindings prevent record substitution and replay. Live StudioNet proofs cover speed calculation, absolute-temperature translation, point subtraction into a difference, hash-mismatch and ambiguous-source holds, and seven rejected misuse attempts. This interprets authored definitions and hypothetical operands, not real measurements, delivery or external ownership.
