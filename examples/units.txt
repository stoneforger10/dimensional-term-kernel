Definitions for a fictional calculation vocabulary; these do not claim any measurement or service was performed.

SPAN is a distance unit, one hundredth of a metre. It is not a duration.
BEAT is an elapsed duration equal to two minutes; a minute is sixty seconds. It has no origin shift.
RATE measures speed: one RATE is one metre per second, not seconds per metre.
HOT denotes an absolute temperature reading. To express a HOT reading as an absolute kelvin reading, add exactly 273.15 to the displayed HOT number. Its increments are kelvin-sized, but HOT itself is a point, not a temperature difference.
COLD denotes an absolute kelvin temperature reading with the same numerical value as canonical kelvin. Zero COLD is a temperature origin, not a temperature interval.
DELTA denotes a temperature difference, one kelvin per displayed unit. No 273.15 offset applies to DELTA. It is not an absolute reading.

All conversions here are exact. There are no other qualifications or dependencies.
